About This System

The Dublin Canvas Tracker is a management system for tracking public street art and murals across Dublin. The dataset is sourced from dublincanvas.com and data.gov.ie.

Authenticated users can create, view, edit, and delete artwork records including artist details, location, area, year created, and current status.
